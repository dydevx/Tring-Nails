# Tring Nails & Beauty Design System

## Direction

An image-led salon website inspired by BeeNail Aylesbury's generous white space, prominent header, full-height photographic hero, service browsing and booking rhythm. The execution remains specific to Tring Nails & Beauty.

## Colour

- Canvas: `#fff9fc`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Pink: `#c95f96`
- Deep rose: `#9d3f70`
- Plum ink: `#351f2d`
- Muted plum: `#705965`
- Pale pink: `#fdebf4`
- Divider: `#ecd6e2`

Pink is the primary action colour. Deep rose anchors promotional blocks and active states, while plum keeps text and high-contrast areas refined.

## Typography

Nunito matches the reference site's friendly, rounded typography across headlines, navigation, body copy, pricing and forms. Headings use balanced wrapping and never exceed 96px.

## Layout

The desktop header is spacious and logo-led. The hero is a full-bleed photographic stage with a readable left-aligned overlay. Main sections use generous vertical spacing and a maximum readable content width. Service and booking controls echo the reference site's clear category navigation without copying its implementation.

## Components

- Buttons: full-pill, champagne fill or brown outline, minimum 44px height.
- Cards: 12 to 16px radii, using either a subtle shadow or border, never both.
- Service cards: image first, concise detail, visible starting price and booking action.
- Price groups: two columns on desktop and native-feeling accordions on mobile.
- Booking: four explicit steps with category filters and inline validation.
- Gallery: clean square/portrait rhythm with lightbox and swipe support.

## Motion

Use gentle hover lift and short content reveals only. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and content remains visible without JavaScript.
