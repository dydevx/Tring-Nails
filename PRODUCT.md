# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local residents, students and visitors in and around Tring who want to compare nail treatments, understand prices and request an appointment quickly on mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Present Tring Nails & Beauty as a professional, welcoming local salon and turn browsing into WhatsApp or telephone appointment requests without implying automatic confirmation or online payment.

## Brand Personality

Polished, warm and attentive. The experience should feel feminine and relaxing without becoming flashy, childish or overly ornate.

## Anti-references

Do not copy BeeNail branding, logo, imagery or wording. Avoid neon colours, generic spa stock imagery, excessive animation, glassmorphism, fake booking confirmation, online payment UI and template-like card repetition.

## Design Principles

- Let real salon work lead the experience.
- Make prices and booking choices easy to scan.
- Keep every conversion path honest about appointment confirmation.
- Use the BeeNail site as a structural and interaction reference, not a brand source.
- Prioritise mobile contact and booking actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, meaningful alt text, reduced-motion support, clearly labelled form validation and touch targets of at least 44 pixels.
