Welcome to Tring
Nails & Beauty

Tring Nails & Beauty is a stylish salon in the heart of Hertfordshire, offering professional nail and massage services in a modern, welcoming and relaxing environment.

Our team specialises in acrylic extensions, BIAB builder gel, gel polish, spa manicures, pedicures, bespoke nail art and relaxing massage treatments tailored to you.

We use premium-quality products and maintain excellent hygiene standards to make every visit comfortable, safe and enjoyable. Whether you are preparing for a special occasion or simply treating yourself, we are dedicated to delivering polished results with friendly and attentive service.
